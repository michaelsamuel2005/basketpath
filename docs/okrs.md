# OKRs for a search, browse and checkout product team

_Generated by `python -m basketpath.build` from baselines measured in the analysis window. Targets are proposals to agree with the product team, not commitments. Metric definitions: [metrics.md](metrics.md)._

## Objective 1: Help shoppers find the right product quickly

- **KR1** Raise the share of search sessions that go on to see products from 47.3% to 52.0% (10% relative).
- **KR2** Raise the share of product-viewing sessions that add to basket from 27.3% to 30.1% (10% relative).

## Objective 2: Make finishing the shop effortless on every device

- **KR3** Hold mobile checkout completion at or above 47.4% (desktop: 45.2%).
- **KR4** Raise purchase events carrying a valid transaction ID to at least 99.5% (currently 84.08%), so every order can be counted once.

## Health metrics: must not get worse while the KRs move

- Session conversion: 1.29%
- Engaged-session share: 90.2%
- High-severity tracking-audit failures: zero
